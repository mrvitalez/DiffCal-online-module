Practise the Chain Rule on powers, trigonometric functions and nested compositions, then use implicit differentiation for slopes, tangent lines and a second derivative. Type answers in the syntax shown, for example (x^2+1)^3 or -x/y.

Scoring and how to type answers

8 graded items, 1 point each, plus a short reflection. Write your solution in each solution box, then type your final answer. Right after submitting you will see your score and which items are correct or wrong.

  • Number answers: a decimal with at least 3 significant figures (0.785) or an exact value (pi/4, 3/8, 2ln(3), sqrt(2)/2).
  • Formula answers: type the expression, e.g. x^2 sin(x), (1/3)e^(3x), ln|x+1|, sec^2(x), arctan(x). Use ^ for powers, sqrt( ) for roots and pi for π. For an indefinite integral add + C. Any correct equivalent form is accepted. The box below the answer shows how your answer was read.
