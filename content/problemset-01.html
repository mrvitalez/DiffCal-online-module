✎Problems

  1. (§1.1) Find the domain and range of \(f(x) = \sqrt{9 - x^2} + 1\). Then find the domain of \(g(x) = \dfrac{x + 1}{x^2 - 2x - 8}\).

  2. (§1.1) Decide whether each function is even, odd or neither, and justify your answer algebraically: (a) \(f(x) = \dfrac{x^3}{x^2 + 1}\) (b) \(g(x) = x^2 - |x|\) (c) \(h(x) = x^2 + x\).

  3. (§1.1, applied) An open box is made from a 20 cm by 30 cm sheet of cardboard by cutting equal squares of side \(x\) cm from each corner and folding up the sides. Express the volume \(V\) of the box as a function of \(x\), and state the domain that makes sense in this situation.

  4. (§1.2, applied) A plumber charges a fixed call-out fee of 500 pesos plus 350 pesos per hour of work. (a) Write the cost \(C\) as a function of the number of hours \(t\). (b) What do the slope and the \(C\)-intercept mean? (c) How much does a 2.5-hour job cost? (d) A customer paid 2600 pesos. How long did the job take?

  5. (§1.2) Classify each function as linear, polynomial (state the degree), power, rational, algebraic, trigonometric, exponential or logarithmic. Some fit more than one type; give the most specific one. (a) \(y = x^5 - 3x\) (b) \(y = \dfrac{x^2 + 1}{x - 4}\) (c) \(y = x^{3/4}\) (d) \(y = \dfrac{\sqrt{x}}{1 + \sqrt{x}}\) (e) \(y = 3^x\) (f) \(y = \sin 2x\).

  6. (§1.3) Starting from the graph of \(y = |x|\), describe in order the transformations that give \(y = 3 - 2|x + 1|\), and sketch it. State its vertex, its range and where it is increasing. Then state the period of \(y = \cos 3x\).

  7. (§1.3) Let \(f(x) = x^2 - 1\) and \(g(x) = \sqrt{x + 2}\). Find (a) \(f \circ g\), (b) \(g \circ f\), (c) \(f \circ f\), and state the domain of each.

  8. (§1.4) Let \(P(1, 2)\) be a point on the curve \(y = 2/x\). (a) Find the slope of the secant line \(PQ\) where \(Q\) has \(x\)-coordinate \(1 + h\), simplified. (b) Compute the secant slopes for \(h = 0.5, 0.1, -0.1\). (c) Find the slope of the tangent line at \(P\) and its equation.

  9. (§1.5) (a) Find \(\lim_{x \to 3^-} \dfrac{|x - 3|}{x - 3}\), \(\lim_{x \to 3^+} \dfrac{|x - 3|}{x - 3}\), and decide whether \(\lim_{x \to 3} \dfrac{|x - 3|}{x - 3}\) exists. (b) Find \(\lim_{x \to -1^+} \dfrac{2x}{x + 1}\). (c) Find all vertical asymptotes of \(y = \dfrac{x^2 - 1}{x^2 + x - 6}\) and the behaviour on each side of them.

  10. (§1.6) Evaluate each limit, showing the algebra: (a) \(\displaystyle\lim_{x \to 5} \frac{x^2 - 25}{x^2 - 3x - 10}\) (b) \(\displaystyle\lim_{x \to 0} \frac{\sqrt{1 + x} - \sqrt{1 - x}}{x}\) (c) \(\displaystyle\lim_{h \to 0} \frac{\frac{1}{3 + h} - \frac13}{h}\).

  11. (§1.6, prove) Use the Squeeze Theorem to prove that \(\displaystyle\lim_{x \to 0} x^4 \sin\!\left(\frac{2}{x}\right) = 0\). Explain why the Product Law cannot be used instead.

  12. (§1.7) (a) For \(\lim_{x \to 4} (6 - 2x) = -2\), find the largest \(\delta\) that works when \(\varepsilon = 0.1\). (b) Write an ε–δ proof of this limit for a general \(\varepsilon > 0\). (c) Prove that \(\lim_{x \to 1} (x^2 + 3x) = 4\) using the ε–δ definition.

  13. (§1.8) (a) Where is \(f(x) = \dfrac{x^2 - x - 2}{x^2 - 4}\) discontinuous? Classify each discontinuity as removable, jump or infinite, and if it is removable, say how to redefine \(f\) to remove it. (b) Find the constant \(k\) that makes \(g(x) = \begin{cases} kx + 3 & x < 1 \\ x^2 + 2k & x \ge 1 \end{cases}\) continuous everywhere.

  14. (§1.8, explain) (a) Use the Intermediate Value Theorem to show that the equation \(\cos x = x\) has a solution in \((0, \pi/2)\). State clearly which function you use and why it is continuous. (b) A hiker starts up a mountain trail at 6:00 a.m. and reaches the top at 4:00 p.m. The next day she starts down the same trail at 6:00 a.m. and reaches the bottom at 4:00 p.m. Explain, using the IVT, why there is a point on the trail that she passes at exactly the same time of day on both days.
