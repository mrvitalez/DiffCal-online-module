When a problem says “use the definition”, you must work from the limit of the difference quotient, not the shortcut rules. Angles are in radians, and answers should be exact unless a decimal is asked for.

✎Problems

  1. Let \(f(x) = 3x^2 - x + 4\). Use the limit definition to find \(f'(2)\), and then write the equation of the tangent line to \(y = f(x)\) at \(x = 2\).

  2. Use the definition of the derivative to find \(f'(x)\) for \(f(x) = \sqrt{3 - x}\). State the domain of \(f\) and of \(f'\).

  3. Use the definition of the derivative to find \(g'(x)\) for \(g(x) = \dfrac{x}{x + 4}\).

  4. A stone is dropped from a bridge 45 m above a river. Its height above the water after \(t\) seconds is \(H(t) = 45 - 4.9t^2\) metres. (a) Find the average velocity over \([1, 2]\). (b) Use the definition to find the instantaneous velocity at \(t = 2\). (c) When does the stone hit the water, and how fast is it moving then? Give decimals to two places.

  5. Let \(f(x) = x^2\) for \(x \le 1\) and \(f(x) = 2x - 1\) for \(x \gt 1\); let \(g(x) = x^2\) for \(x \le 1\) and \(g(x) = 3x - 2\) for \(x \gt 1\). For each function, decide whether it is continuous at \(x = 1\) and whether it is differentiable at \(x = 1\). Justify your answers with one-sided limits of the difference quotient.

  6. Prove that if \(f\) is differentiable at \(a\), then \(f\) is continuous at \(a\). Then explain why the function \(y = |x - 2|\) shows that the converse is false.

  7. Let \(f(x) = x^3 - 3x\). (a) Find \(f'(x)\). (b) On one set of axes, sketch \(f\) and \(f'\) for \(-2 \le x \le 2\). (c) Explain, using your sketch, how the zeros and the sign of \(f'\) match the shape of the graph of \(f\).

  8. Find the derivative of \(y = (x^2 + 3)(2x^3 - x)\) in two ways: with the Product Rule, and by expanding first. Show that your answers agree.

  9. Differentiate \(y = \dfrac{x^2 - 1}{x^2 + x + 1}\) and simplify the numerator.

  10. Find the equations of the tangent line and the normal line to \(y = \dfrac{x + 1}{\sqrt{x}}\) at the point where \(x = 4\).

  11. For the curve \(y = x^3 - 3x^2 - 9x + 2\): (a) find all points where the tangent line is horizontal; (b) find all points where the tangent line is parallel to the line \(y = -9x + 1\).

  12. Differentiate: (a) \(y = x^3\cos x\); (b) \(y = \dfrac{\sin x}{1 - \cos x}\) (simplify fully); (c) \(y = \sec x\tan x\).

  13. Evaluate each limit: (a) \(\displaystyle\lim_{x \to 0}\frac{\sin 5x}{2x}\); (b) \(\displaystyle\lim_{x \to 0}\frac{\tan 4x}{x}\); (c) \(\displaystyle\lim_{x \to 0}\frac{1 - \cos x}{x\sin x}\).

  14. A mass on a spring moves up and down with position \(s(t) = 3\cos t - 4\sin t\) centimetres after \(t\) seconds. (a) Find the velocity and acceleration functions. (b) Find the velocity and acceleration at \(t = \pi/2\). (c) Show that \(a(t) = -s(t)\) for every \(t\), and explain what this says about the direction of the acceleration.

  15. Using the limit definition, the identity \(\cos(x + h) = \cos x\cos h - \sin x\sin h\), and the two special trigonometric limits, prove that \(\dfrac{d}{dx}(\cos x) = -\sin x\). Then use the Quotient Rule to prove \(\dfrac{d}{dx}(\cot x) = -\csc^2 x\).
