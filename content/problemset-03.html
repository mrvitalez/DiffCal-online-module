✎Problems

  1. (§2.5) Differentiate. (a) \(y = (2x^2 - 7)^5\)   (b) \(y = \dfrac{1}{\sqrt{x^2 + 4x}}\)   (c) \(y = \sin(x^2 + 3x)\)

  2. (§2.5) Differentiate. Identify each layer before you start. (a) \(y = \tan^3(2x)\)   (b) \(y = \sec\sqrt{x}\)   (c) \(y = \sin(\cos 4x)\)

  3. (§2.5) Differentiate and simplify to a factored form. (a) \(y = x^3(1 - 2x)^4\)   (b) \(y = \left(\dfrac{x - 1}{x + 3}\right)^5\)

  4. (§2.5) The table gives values of \(f\), \(g\) and their derivatives.

    \(x\)\(f(x)\)\(f'(x)\)\(g(x)\)\(g'(x)\)
    13−225
    2143−1
    32716

    Find (a) \(h'(1)\) if \(h(x) = f(g(x))\); (b) \(k'(1)\) if \(k(x) = g(f(x))\); (c) \(F'(1)\) if \(F(x) = [f(x)]^3\); (d) \(G'(1)\) if \(G(x) = f(x^2)\).

  5. (§2.5) (a) Find an equation of the tangent line to \(y = \sqrt{1 + x^3}\) at the point \((2, 3)\). (b) Find all \(x\) in \([0, 2\pi]\) where the curve \(y = 2\sin x + \cos^2 x\) has a horizontal tangent.

  6. (§2.5, explain/prove) (a) A function \(f\) is even if \(f(-x) = f(x)\) for all \(x\). Use the Chain Rule to prove that the derivative of a differentiable even function is odd, that is, \(f'(-x) = -f'(x)\). (b) Calculus formulas for trigonometric functions assume radians. If \(x\) is measured in degrees, \(\sin(x^\circ) = \sin\!\left(\dfrac{\pi x}{180}\right)\). Find \(\dfrac{d}{dx}\sin(x^\circ)\) and explain in one or two sentences why calculus uses radians.

  7. (§2.6) Find \(dy/dx\) by implicit differentiation. (a) \(x^3 - 2xy^2 + y^4 = 5\)   (b) \(\sqrt{x + y} = 1 + x^2 y\)   (c) \(y\cos x = x^2 + y^2\)

  8. (§2.6) Consider the ellipse \(x^2 - xy + y^2 = 7\). (a) Check that \((-1, 2)\) is on the curve and find the tangent line there. (b) Find all points on the curve where the tangent line is horizontal.

  9. (§2.6) For the curve \(x^3 + y^3 = 8\), find \(y'\) and then show that \(y'' = -\dfrac{16x}{y^5}\).

  10. (§2.7) A particle moves along a line with position \(s(t) = t^3 - 12t^2 + 36t\) metres, \(0 \le t \le 8\) seconds. (a) Find \(v(t)\) and \(a(t)\). (b) When is the particle at rest? (c) Find the total distance travelled from \(t = 0\) to \(t = 8\). (d) On which time intervals is it speeding up?

  11. (§2.7, applied) A small bakery estimates that the cost of baking \(x\) loaves per day is \(C(x) = 4000 + 15x - 0.03x^2 + 0.00004x^3\) pesos. (a) Find the marginal cost function. (b) Find \(C'(300)\) and explain its meaning in words, with units. (c) Compute the actual cost of the 301st loaf, \(C(301) - C(300)\), and compare.

  12. (§2.7, applied) In a chemical reaction the concentration of a product after \(t\) minutes is \([P] = \dfrac{3t}{2t + 10}\) mol/L. (a) Find the rate of reaction \(d[P]/dt\). (b) Find the rate at \(t = 5\), with units. (c) Is the reaction speeding up or slowing down? What concentration does \([P]\) approach as \(t\) grows?

  13. (§2.8) Air is pumped into a spherical balloon at 50 cm³/s. (a) How fast is the radius increasing when the diameter is 20 cm? (b) How fast is the surface area \(S = 4\pi r^2\) increasing at that moment?

  14. (§2.8, applied) A kite flies at a constant height of 60 m, and the wind carries it horizontally away from the person flying it at 2 m/s. How fast is the angle \(\theta\) between the string and the ground changing when 100 m of string has been let out? (Assume the string is straight, and measure the height from the person's hand.)

  15. (§2.9) (a) Find the linearization of \(f(x) = \dfrac{1}{\sqrt{1 + 2x}}\) at \(a = 0\) and use it to estimate \(\dfrac{1}{\sqrt{1.1}}\). (b) Use a linear approximation to estimate \(\sqrt[3]{63.7}\). (c) The radius of a circular disk is measured as 24 cm with a maximum error of 0.2 cm. Use differentials to estimate the maximum error and the percentage error in the computed area. (d) Explain why your estimate in (b) is slightly too large.
