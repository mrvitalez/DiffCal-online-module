✎Problems

  1. Find all critical numbers of (a) \(f(x) = 2x^3 + 3x^2 - 36x\) and (b) \(g(t) = t^{3/4} - 2t^{1/4}\) (domain \(t \ge 0\)).

  2. Use the Closed Interval Method to find the absolute maximum and minimum values of \(f(x) = x^4 - 2x^2 + 3\) on \([-2, 3]\).

  3. Find the absolute maximum and minimum values of \(f(x) = \dfrac{x}{x^2 + 4}\) on \([0, 3]\).

  4. Find the absolute maximum and minimum values of \(f(t) = 2\cos t + \sin 2t\) on \([0, \pi/2]\).

  5. Verify that \(f(x) = x^3 - 4x\) satisfies the three hypotheses of Rolle's Theorem on \([0, 2]\). Then find every number \(c\) that satisfies the conclusion.

  6. Verify that \(f(x) = \sqrt{x + 1}\) satisfies the hypotheses of the Mean Value Theorem on \([0, 3]\), and find the number \(c\) that satisfies the conclusion.

  7. For \(f(x) = x^4 - 4x^3\), find (a) the intervals on which \(f\) is increasing or decreasing, (b) the local extreme values, and (c) the intervals of concavity and the inflection points. Explain why \(x = 0\) is a critical number but not a local extremum.

  8. Use the Second Derivative Test to find the local maximum and minimum values of \(f(x) = \sin x + \cos x\) on \((0, 2\pi)\). Also find the inflection points on this interval.

  9. Evaluate each limit, showing your algebra.
    (a) \(\displaystyle\lim_{x\to\infty}\frac{4x^3 - 2x + 7}{1 - 2x^3}\)   (b) \(\displaystyle\lim_{x\to-\infty}\frac{x + 3}{\sqrt{9x^2 - x}}\)   (c) \(\displaystyle\lim_{x\to\infty}\left(\sqrt{4x^2 + x} - 2x\right)\)   (d) \(\displaystyle\lim_{x\to\infty}\left(x^2 - x^3\right)\)

  10. Find all horizontal and vertical asymptotes of \(y = \dfrac{2x^2 + x - 1}{x^2 + x - 2}\).

  11. A small bakery has fixed costs of ₱5000 per day, and each loaf costs ₱12 to make. The average cost per loaf when \(x\) loaves are made is \(A(x) = \dfrac{5000 + 12x}{x}\). Show that \(A\) is decreasing for \(x \gt 0\), find \(\lim_{x\to\infty} A(x)\), and explain what the limit means.

  12. A bus passes a toll gate at 8:00 a.m. and another toll gate 159 km farther along the expressway at 9:30 a.m. The speed limit is 100 km/h. Use the Mean Value Theorem to show that the bus exceeded the speed limit at some moment. State what you are assuming about the position function.

  13. Show that the equation \(2x + \cos x = 0\) has exactly one real root. (Use the Intermediate Value Theorem for "at least one" and Rolle's Theorem for "at most one".)

  14. Use the Mean Value Theorem to prove that \(|\sin a - \sin b| \le |a - b|\) for all real numbers \(a\) and \(b\).

  15. Let \(f(x) = x^{1/3}(x + 4)\). Find the intervals of increase and decrease, the local extreme values, the intervals of concavity and the inflection points. Describe what happens to the tangent line at \(x = 0\).
