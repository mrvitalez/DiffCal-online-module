✎Problems

  1. Use the checklist A–H to sketch \(f(x) = x^3 - 3x^2 - 9x + 5\). Label all local extreme points and inflection points.

  2. Use the checklist A–H to sketch \(f(x) = \dfrac{x^2 - 1}{x^2 - 9}\). Include all asymptotes, intercepts and extreme values.

  3. Sketch \(f(x) = x + 2\sin x\) on \([0, 2\pi]\). Find the intervals of increase and decrease, the local extreme values, the intervals of concavity and the inflection point.

  4. A rectangular field of area \(2400\text{ m}^2\) is to be fenced and then divided into three equal pens by two fences parallel to one pair of sides. What dimensions use the least total fencing, and how much fencing is needed?

  5. An open-top cylindrical water tank (no lid) must hold \(27\pi\text{ m}^3\). Find the radius and height that use the least material, and the minimum surface area.

  6. Find the point on the parabola \(y = x^2\) that is closest to the point \((6, 3)\), and the shortest distance.

  7. A rectangle is inscribed in a semicircle of radius 5, with one side on the diameter. Find the largest possible area of the rectangle.

  8. Use Newton's method with \(x_1 = 1.6\) to approximate \(\sqrt[4]{7}\). Compute \(x_2\) and \(x_3\), and give \(x_3\) to six decimal places.

  9. Use Newton's method to find the root of \(2\cos x = x\) correct to six decimal places. Explain how you chose your starting value. (Use radians.)

  10. Let \(f(x) = x^3 - 3x + 3\). Explain what goes wrong if you use Newton's method with \(x_1 = 0\). Then choose a better starting value and find the real root to four decimal places.

  11. Find the most general antiderivative of \(f(x) = \dfrac{x^3 + 2\sqrt{x}}{x}\) for \(x > 0\), and check your answer by differentiating.

  12. Find \(f\) if \(f''(x) = 12x^2 - 6\), \(f(0) = 4\) and \(f(1) = 1\).

  13. A car travelling at 25 m/s brakes with constant deceleration and comes to a stop in 50 m. Find the deceleration and the time it takes to stop. (Set up \(a(t) = -k\) and find \(v\) and \(s\) by antidifferentiation.)

  14. Explain why, if \(F'(x) = G'(x)\) for every \(x\) in an interval \(I\), then \(F(x) - G(x)\) is constant on \(I\). Use your explanation to say why \(\sin^2 x\) and \(-\cos^2 x\) are both antiderivatives of the same function, and find the constant by which they differ.
