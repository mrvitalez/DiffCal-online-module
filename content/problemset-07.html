✎Problems

  1. (§6.5) A quantity satisfies \(\dfrac{dy}{dt} = -0.3y\) with \(y(0) = 50\). (a) Write \(y(t)\). (b) Find \(y(4)\). (c) When does \(y\) reach 10?

  2. (§6.5, applied) A bacteria culture grows at a rate proportional to its size. There are 1200 bacteria after 2 hours and 4800 after 5 hours. (a) Find the relative growth rate \(k\). (b) How many bacteria were there initially? (c) When will there be 20 000 bacteria?

  3. (§6.5, applied) A hospital receives 120 mg of iodine-131, which has a half-life of 8 days. (a) Find a formula for the mass remaining after \(t\) days. (b) How much remains after 20 days? (c) After how many days is less than 5 mg left?

  4. (§6.5) A cake comes out of the oven at 175°C and is left in a kitchen at 25°C. After 15 minutes its temperature is 100°C. Using Newton's Law of Cooling, (a) find the temperature after 30 minutes, and (b) find when the cake reaches 40°C.

  5. (§6.5) You deposit ₱20 000 at an annual interest rate of 4.5%. (a) Find the amount after 8 years if interest is compounded quarterly. (b) Find the amount after 8 years if it is compounded continuously. (c) With continuous compounding, how long does it take the deposit to double?

  6. (§6.6) Find the exact value: (a) \(\sin^{-1}\!\left(-\tfrac{\sqrt3}{2}\right)\) (b) \(\cos^{-1}\!\left(-\tfrac12\right)\) (c) \(\tan^{-1}(-1)\) (d) \(\cos\!\left(\sin^{-1}\tfrac{5}{13}\right)\) (e) \(\sin^{-1}\!\left(\sin\tfrac{7\pi}{6}\right)\).

  7. (§6.6) Write each as an algebraic expression in \(x\): (a) \(\sin(\tan^{-1}x)\) (b) \(\cos(2\sin^{-1}x)\), for \(-1 \le x \le 1\).

  8. (§6.6) Differentiate: (a) \(y = \tan^{-1}\sqrt{x}\) (b) \(y = x\sin^{-1}(x^2)\) (c) \(y = \cos^{-1}(1/x)\) for \(x \gt 1\); also find \(y'(3)\) in (c).

  9. (§6.6, prove) Show that \(\sin^{-1}x + \cos^{-1}x = \dfrac{\pi}{2}\) for \(-1 \le x \le 1\). (Hint: differentiate the left side, then evaluate it at one convenient point.)

  10. (§6.7) If \(\tanh x = \tfrac{12}{13}\), find the exact values of \(\cosh x\), \(\sinh x\), \(\operatorname{sech} x\), \(\operatorname{csch} x\) and \(\coth x\).

  11. (§6.7) Differentiate: (a) \(y = \sinh x\cosh 2x\) (b) \(y = \ln(\cosh x)\) (c) \(y = x\sinh x - \cosh x\) (d) \(y = \cosh^{-1}(3x)\) for \(x \gt \tfrac13\).

  12. (§6.7, applied) A power line hangs between two poles 60 m apart. With the origin on the ground halfway between the poles, the line is modelled by \(y = 30\cosh(x/30) - 20\) for \(-30 \le x \le 30\) (in metres). (a) How high is the lowest point of the line? (b) How much does the line sag (height at a pole minus height at the lowest point)? (c) What is the slope of the line where it meets the right-hand pole?

  13. (§6.8) Evaluate each limit, stating the indeterminate form each time you use l'Hospital's Rule: (a) \(\displaystyle\lim_{x\to 1}\frac{x^5 - 1}{x^3 - 1}\) (b) \(\displaystyle\lim_{x\to 0}\frac{\tan x - x}{x^3}\) (c) \(\displaystyle\lim_{x\to\infty}\frac{(\ln x)^2}{x}\) (d) \(\displaystyle\lim_{x\to 0^+}\sin x\,\ln x\).

  14. (§6.8) Evaluate: (a) \(\displaystyle\lim_{x\to 0}\left(\cot x - \frac1x\right)\) (b) \(\displaystyle\lim_{x\to\infty} x^{1/x}\) (c) \(\displaystyle\lim_{x\to 0^+}(1 + 5x)^{2/x}\) (d) \(\displaystyle\lim_{x\to 0^+} x^{x^2}\).

  15. (§6.8, explain) (a) Show that \(\displaystyle\lim_{x\to\infty}\frac{x + \sin x}{x}\) is of type \(\tfrac{\infty}{\infty}\), but that l'Hospital's Rule gives \(\lim_{x\to\infty}(1 + \cos x)\), which does not exist. Does this mean the original limit does not exist? Find it another way. (b) A classmate writes \(\displaystyle\lim_{x\to 0}\frac{x^2}{\sin x} = \lim_{x\to 0}\frac{2x\sin x - x^2\cos x}{\sin^2 x}\). Explain the mistake and find the correct limit.
